Punkenness

SOS Book 1. Out now.

Tom has his future all planned out: first, he'll smash his A-Levels, then he'll read English at Oxford University and be a Booker Prize winning author by the time he turns 30.

But when a young punk with dazzling blue eyes and an aversion to education is seated next to him in class, an unlikely friendship blossoms.

Persuaded to tour the UK with his new friend's band over the summer, Tom experiences danger, true friendship and freedom for the first time, forcing him to question everything he knows about the world and his future in it.

For fans of Heartstopper and the cult classic film Almost Famous.

This book features recreational drug use. It is not recommended for young readers.

Goth Girl

Would you fight for a second chance at first love?

At school Chris and Pippa were never friends. But a secret attraction kept them connected like a thread of invisible string.

Quiet, artistic and different in a way no one could quite put a finger on, Pippa was an easy target for bullies. Young and immature, Chris tried to help in the only way he knew how. He wishes he'd done more.

Five years later, Chris and Pippa meet again at university. The spark is still there. But Pippa is dealing with a different kind of bully, now. And she is dead-set on keeping them apart.

For readers aged 16 and above. Contains the loss of a family member, eating disorders and bullying.
